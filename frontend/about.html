About CyberScanner

A web-based cybersecurity scanning tool.

Upload a ZIP archive and CyberScanner will inspect every file inside it, flag suspicious content, and score the findings by severity. Track your scan history and visualize threat trends from the dashboard.

Built with Python (Flask) on the backend and plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript on the frontend.
